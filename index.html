Japan Trip Picks

Places to eat, see, and do, picked for a trip for two. Each entry links to its website and Google Maps.

Mid-October to November is autumn-foliage season, so Kyoto temples get busy. Go early in the morning. Popular restaurants often need a reservation; check hours before going.

Egg labels are a general guide, not a guarantee. Recipes vary and shared fryers or grills are common, so show the allergy card and confirm with staff every time: 「卵アレルギーがあります。卵を含む料理は食べられません。」 (I have an egg allergy and cannot eat dishes that contain egg.)
